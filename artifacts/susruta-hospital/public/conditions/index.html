Our Approach to Chronic Disease

In Ayurveda, disease arises from an imbalance in the three doshas (Vata, Pitta, Kapha), accumulation of Ama (toxins), and disruption of the body-mind-spirit connection. Dr. P. Murali Krishna uses Nadi Pariksha (pulse diagnosis) and Prakriti analysis to identify the root cause in each patient and design a personalised treatment protocol.

Ayurvedic treatment for chronic conditions typically combines herbal medicines, Panchakarma detoxification, dietary guidance, and lifestyle modifications — creating lasting change rather than temporary symptom suppression.

The Ayurvedic Advantage for Chronic Conditions

Many patients come to Susruta Hospital after years of managing chronic conditions with allopathic medicines — experiencing side effects without a lasting cure. Ayurveda offers a different path:

  • Root cause treatment — addressing the doshic imbalance that caused the disease, not just suppressing symptoms
  • No harmful side effects — when prescribed by a qualified physician, Ayurvedic medicines are safe for long-term use
  • Complementary approach — Ayurvedic treatment can work alongside your existing allopathic medications under proper supervision
  • Holistic management — diet, lifestyle, sleep, and mental wellbeing are all addressed as part of the treatment protocol
  • Prevention — once the condition is managed, Ayurveda helps prevent recurrence through Rasayana therapies
