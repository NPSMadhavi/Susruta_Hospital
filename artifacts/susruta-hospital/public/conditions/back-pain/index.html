Back Pain in Ayurveda

Back pain conditions are classified in Ayurveda as Kati Shoola (lower back pain), Gridhrasi (sciatica — named after the vulture's gait due to limping), and Kati Vata (lumbar Vata disorders including spondylosis and disc problems).

All these are primarily Vata disorders — aggravated by excessive sitting or standing, sedentary lifestyle, irregular sleep, stress, dry foods, and cold environments. Vata becomes lodged in the lumbar region, causing dryness and degeneration of the discs and vertebrae.

The Ayurvedic approach is to directly nourish and lubricate the affected structures, restore Vata balance, and strengthen the supporting muscles and ligaments — producing both immediate pain relief and long-term structural improvement.

Conditions We Treat

Lower Back Pain (Kati Shoola)

Acute and chronic lower back pain from muscle strain, postural problems, or disc issues. Kati Basti and Abhyanga provide rapid, lasting relief.

Sciatica (Gridhrasi)

Radiating pain from the lower back down the leg. Basti and Kati Basti are the most effective Ayurvedic treatments for sciatic nerve compression.

Disc Prolapse (PLID)

Lumbar disc herniation causing nerve compression. Basti and Kati Basti reduce inflammation and disc pressure — often avoiding the need for surgery.

Lumbar Spondylosis

Degenerative changes in lumbar vertebrae — Basti and Rasayana medicines slow degeneration, reduce pain, and improve mobility.

Cervical Spondylosis

Neck pain and stiffness — Greeva Basti (localised neck oil treatment) and Nasya are highly effective for cervical spine conditions.

Ankylosing Spondylitis

Chronic spinal inflammation — Basti and anti-Ama protocols reduce the inflammatory process and prevent progressive spinal fusion.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Can Ayurveda cure disc prolapse without surgery?
    Ayurvedic treatment — particularly Basti and Kati Basti — can significantly reduce symptoms of disc prolapse in many cases, avoiding surgery. Results depend on the degree of prolapse and duration of symptoms. Dr. P. Murali Krishna will review your MRI reports and advise on suitability.
  • What is Kati Basti and how does it help?
    Kati Basti is a localised Ayurvedic treatment where warm medicated oil is held over the lower back for 30–45 minutes using a dough ring. The oil penetrates the muscles, ligaments, and discs, reducing inflammation, lubricating the spinal joints, and relieving nerve compression. It is an external treatment, not an enema.
  • Is Ayurvedic treatment safe for elderly patients with back pain?
    Yes. Ayurvedic treatments like Kati Basti, Abhyanga, and Basti are gentle and well-tolerated even by elderly patients. They are often the preferred option when surgical intervention carries too much risk. Treatment is adapted for the patient's age, strength, and condition.
  • How long does back pain treatment take?
    Acute back pain (recent onset) often responds within 5–10 sessions of Kati Basti. Chronic conditions like disc prolapse or spondylosis require a complete Panchakarma course (14–28 days) and continued herbal medicines for 2–3 months. Most patients experience significant improvement in pain and mobility within the first 2 weeks.
