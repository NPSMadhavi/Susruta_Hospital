Skin Disorders in Ayurveda — Kushtha

Ayurveda classifies chronic skin disorders under Kushtha (कुष्ठ) — a group of 18 conditions ranging from mild (Kshudra Kushtha) to major (Maha Kushtha). All Kushtha conditions involve vitiation of the three doshas, the blood (Rakta), skin (Twacha), and sweat channels (Svedavaha Srotas).

The root cause in Ayurvedic dermatology is almost always internal — liver dysfunction, blood toxins (Rakta Dushti), and digestive imbalances — manifesting on the skin surface. This is why topical treatments alone rarely produce lasting results for chronic skin conditions.

Conditions We Treat

Psoriasis (Ekakushtha / Kitibha)

Chronic autoimmune skin condition with scaly plaques. Ayurvedic treatment through Virechana, Raktamokshana, and blood-purifying herbal formulations produces significant and lasting improvement.

Eczema / Atopic Dermatitis

Itchy, inflamed skin — treated by identifying and eliminating the doshic trigger, purifying blood and skin channels, and external application of medicated oils.

Urticaria (Sheetapitta)

Allergic skin reaction with hives and itching — primarily a Vata-Pitta condition treated with anti-allergic herbs and Pitta-pacifying Panchakarma.

Vitiligo (Shvitra)

Loss of skin pigmentation — Ayurveda uses Bakuchi (Psoralea), blood purification, and specific external applications to stimulate melanocyte activity.

Acne (Mukhadushika)

Pitta and Kapha-predominant skin disorder — internal blood purification and Virechana combined with herbal face applications.

Fungal Infections & Ringworm

Anti-fungal Ayurvedic formulations including Gandhak (Sulphur) preparations and Neem-based medicines are highly effective.

The Ayurvedic Treatment Approach for Skin

  1. Identifying Root CauseAssessment of liver function, digestive fire (Agni), blood quality (Rakta), and doshic pattern through Nadi Pariksha and detailed case history.
  2. Internal CleansingVirechana (purgation) to purify the liver and blood — the cornerstone of Ayurvedic dermatology. Raktamokshana (bloodletting) for specific inflammatory skin conditions.
  3. Rakta Shodhana (Blood Purification)Herbal medicines that purify the blood: Manjistha (Rubia cordifolia), Neem, Khadira, Sariva, Guduchi, Haridra (Turmeric), Amla — prescribed as a specific formulation for each skin condition.
  4. External ApplicationsMedicated oils, herbal pastes (Lepa), and Takradhara (medicated buttermilk) for specific conditions. External treatments support but do not replace internal cleansing.
  5. Dietary ProtocolStrict dietary guidelines are essential for skin conditions: avoid incompatible food combinations (Viruddha Ahara), dairy with fish, heating and cooling foods together. A Pitta-pacifying diet is prescribed.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Can Ayurveda permanently cure psoriasis?
    Ayurveda can achieve significant and long-lasting remission in psoriasis through blood purification, Virechana, and herbal medicines. The key is addressing the root cause — liver toxins and blood vitiation — rather than just suppressing skin symptoms. Results depend on the severity, duration, and strict adherence to dietary guidelines. Regular follow-up is needed.
  • Are Ayurvedic medicines safe for long-term skin treatment?
    When prescribed by a qualified physician, Ayurvedic medicines for skin conditions are safe for long-term use with minimal side effects — unlike prolonged use of steroids and immunosuppressants. Dr. P. Murali Krishna carefully selects formulations appropriate for long-term management.
  • Is diet important in Ayurvedic skin treatment?
    Diet is critically important. Many skin conditions are directly aggravated by incompatible food combinations (Viruddha Ahara) such as milk with fish or sour foods, or by excess Pitta-aggravating foods. Strict dietary discipline significantly accelerates recovery and prevents recurrence.
