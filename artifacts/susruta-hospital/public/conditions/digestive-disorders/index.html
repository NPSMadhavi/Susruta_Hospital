Digestion in Ayurveda — Agni

Ayurveda considers Agni (digestive fire) the most fundamental factor in health. When Agni functions optimally, digestion is complete, nutrients are absorbed efficiently, and Ama (undigested residue) is not produced. When Agni is impaired — whether weakened (Mandagni), irregular (Vishamagni), or overactive (Tikshna Agni) — disease follows.

"Sarva roga api mandagni" — "All diseases are rooted in impaired digestive fire." — Charaka Samhita

This is why Ayurvedic treatment for any chronic disease begins with assessing and restoring digestive function. At Susruta Hospital, digestive health is addressed as a foundation for all treatment plans, not just for GI disorders.

Conditions We Treat

IBS (Grahani)

Irritable Bowel Syndrome with alternating constipation and diarrhoea — treated through Agni restoration, Basti, and specific herbal formulations like Kutajarishta.

Chronic Constipation

Vata-predominant condition — Basti (medicated enema) directly addresses the colon, while dietary and lifestyle changes prevent recurrence.

Gastritis & Hyperacidity (Amlapitta)

Excess Pitta in the stomach — Virechana, alkaline diet, and Pitta-pacifying herbal medicines (Avipattikar Churna, Shatavari) provide relief.

Liver Disorders

Fatty liver, hepatitis, and liver function impairment — Virechana and liver-specific herbs (Kutki, Bhumyamalaki, Kalmegh) restore liver function.

Haemorrhoids (Arsha)

Piles and anal fissures — specific Basti formulations, Kshara Karma (cauterisation), and herbal medicines without surgery in most cases.

Inflammatory Bowel Disease

Crohn's disease and ulcerative colitis — Basti with anti-inflammatory formulations and mucous-membrane-healing herbs provide significant relief.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Can Ayurveda permanently cure IBS?
    Ayurveda addresses IBS (Grahani) at the root — the impaired Agni and Vata imbalance in the colon — rather than just managing symptoms. With a complete course of Panchakarma, personalised herbal medicines, and adherence to dietary guidelines, many patients achieve long-lasting remission from IBS.
  • Is Ayurvedic treatment for liver disease safe alongside existing medications?
    When prescribed by a qualified physician, Ayurvedic liver medicines like Kutki, Bhumyamalaki, and Kalmegh are safe and complement conventional hepatology treatment. However, always disclose all medications to Dr. P. Murali Krishna to check for any interactions. Regular liver function tests are recommended during treatment.
  • What dietary changes help digestive disorders in Ayurveda?
    The specific diet depends on your Agni type and doshic imbalance. Generally: eat at regular times, avoid late meals, avoid cold/raw food and ice water, eat warm easily digestible food, use digestive spices (cumin, ginger, fennel), avoid eating incompatible foods together, and do not overeat. Dr. P. Murali Krishna provides detailed personalised dietary guidance.
