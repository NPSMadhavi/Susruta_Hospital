What is Abhyanga?

Abhyanga (Sanskrit: अभ्यंग) is the classical Ayurvedic full-body oil massage. Unlike a simple relaxation massage, Abhyanga is a therapeutic procedure using specific medicated oils selected for the patient's Prakriti and health condition. The oil is applied with rhythmic strokes along the direction of hair follicles to penetrate deep into the tissues.

According to classical Ayurvedic texts, regular Abhyanga counteracts the effects of ageing, nourishes all seven body tissues (Saptadhatu), improves vision, bestows good sleep, strengthens the body's tolerance, and promotes longevity.

In the context of Panchakarma, Abhyanga is the primary Bahya Snehana (external oleation) procedure — the essential preparatory step that loosens deep-seated toxins from the tissues and channels them towards the digestive tract for elimination.

Benefits of Abhyanga

Improved Circulation

Warm medicated oil and rhythmic massage stimulate the lymphatic and circulatory systems, improving blood flow to peripheral tissues.

Joint & Muscle Health

Reduces Vata in joints and muscles — relieves stiffness, pain, and inflammation in arthritis, fibromyalgia, and muscle disorders.

Skin Nourishment

Medicated oils deeply moisturise and nourish the skin, improving tone, lustre, and elasticity. Beneficial for dry, ageing, and stressed skin.

Stress & Anxiety Relief

The combination of warm oil and therapeutic touch activates the parasympathetic nervous system, reducing cortisol and inducing deep relaxation.

Detoxification Support

As the main Purva Karma procedure, Abhyanga loosens Ama (toxins) from the tissues, preparing the body for deeper Panchakarma cleansing.

Better Sleep

Regular Abhyanga, especially before bed, significantly improves sleep quality by calming Vata and the nervous system.

The Role of Medicated Oils

The therapeutic effect of Abhyanga depends greatly on the selection of the appropriate medicated oil. Dr. P. Murali Krishna prescribes the correct oil based on your dosha constitution and specific condition:

  • Sesame oil (Tila Taila) — the most commonly used base; warming, nourishing, excellent for Vata disorders
  • Mahanarayan Taila — classical formulation for joint pain, arthritis, and musculoskeletal disorders
  • Ksheerabala Taila — calming, nourishing; used for neurological conditions and Vata imbalances
  • Dhanwantaram Taila — postpartum care, joint disorders, neurological conditions
  • Brahmi Taila — calming and nourishing for the nervous system and brain
  • Coconut oil — cooling; used for Pitta-predominant conditions and inflammatory skin disorders
