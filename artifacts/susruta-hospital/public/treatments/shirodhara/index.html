What is Shirodhara?

Shirodhara (Sanskrit: शिरोधारा — Shiro = head, Dhara = stream/flow) is one of the most distinctive and deeply effective Ayurvedic therapies. A continuous, gentle stream of warm medicated oil is poured onto the Ajna Marma — the area of the forehead corresponding to the third eye — from a height of about 10 cm, for 30–60 minutes.

The rhythmic, uninterrupted flow creates a profound meditative state, calming the hypothalamus and the entire central nervous system. Patients often describe it as a deeply peaceful experience unlike any other.

Conditions Treated by Shirodhara

Stress & Anxiety

Shirodhara is the most effective Ayurvedic therapy for chronic stress, generalised anxiety, and mental fatigue. It directly pacifies Vata and Pitta in the head and mind.

Insomnia

Patients with chronic insomnia often experience significant improvement after a course of 7–14 Shirodhara sessions, especially when combined with Abhyanga.

Migraine & Headaches

Highly effective for tension headaches, migraine, and cluster headaches by reducing Pitta-Vata in the nervous system and calming hyperactive neural pathways.

Hypertension

Regular Shirodhara helps lower elevated blood pressure by reducing sympathetic nervous system overactivity and promoting deep relaxation.

Depression & Mental Fatigue

Helps lift mental fog, improve mood, and restore emotional balance — particularly useful in combination with Ayurvedic herbal medicines.

Neurological Conditions

Used as a supportive therapy for epilepsy, Bell's palsy, tinnitus, and post-stroke recovery in conjunction with other Panchakarma procedures.

How Shirodhara is Performed

  1. AssessmentDr. P. Murali Krishna assesses your Prakriti and current condition to select the appropriate medicated oil — sesame, Brahmi, Ksheerabala, or other specialised formulations.
  2. Preparatory AbhyangaA brief warm oil massage of the head, neck, and shoulders to prepare the tissues and improve absorption of the medicated oil.
  3. Shirodhara ProcedureYou lie comfortably on the treatment table. A specially designed vessel (Dhara Patra) is filled with warm medicated oil and positioned above the forehead. The oil flows in a continuous, gentle stream over the forehead and scalp for 30–60 minutes.
  4. Rest PeriodFollowing the treatment, a brief rest period of 15–20 minutes is recommended to allow the nervous system to settle and absorb the full benefit of the therapy.
  5. Course of TreatmentA single session provides noticeable relaxation, but a course of 7–14 sessions is recommended for chronic conditions. Dr. P. Murali Krishna will prescribe the appropriate number of sessions based on your condition.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • How long does a Shirodhara session last?
    A session lasts 45–60 minutes including the preparatory massage. A course of 7–14 sessions is usually recommended for chronic conditions.
  • Is Shirodhara safe?
    Shirodhara is very safe when performed by a qualified practitioner with the correct oil for your constitution. At Susruta Hospital, all sessions are prescribed and overseen by Dr. P. Murali Krishna.
  • Can I have Shirodhara if I have high blood pressure?
    Shirodhara can be very beneficial for hypertension, but the prescription must be made after a proper assessment. Please inform Dr. P. Murali Krishna of all your current medications and health conditions during your consultation.
  • What should I do after a Shirodhara session?
    Rest for at least 30 minutes. Avoid washing your hair immediately — let the oil remain for a few hours. Keep warm, avoid cold air, and eat a light meal. Avoid stressful activities on the day of treatment.
