What is Basti?

Basti (Sanskrit: बस्ति) is the most important of the five Panchakarma procedures. Charaka, the ancient Ayurvedic authority, called Basti the "Ardha Chikitsa" — half of all medical treatment — because Vata is the root cause of the majority of diseases, and Basti is the primary therapy for pacifying Vata dosha.

Medicated substances — either warm sesame oil (Anuvasana Basti) or herbal decoctions mixed with oil and honey (Niruha/Asthapana Basti) — are administered through the rectum into the colon. The colon is the primary seat of Vata in the body. By directly treating the colon, Basti corrects Vata imbalances throughout the entire body.

Types of Basti

Anuvasana Basti

Oil Basti — warm medicated oil retained in the colon. Primarily nourishing and lubricating. Safe for regular use, even daily. Best for chronic Vata conditions, weakness, and depletion.

Niruha Basti (Asthapana)

Decoction Basti — complex herbal mixture administered and expelled. Deep cleansing action. Used for specific disease conditions. Typically alternated with Anuvasana Basti in a Basti course.

Kati Basti

A localised treatment where warm medicated oil is pooled over the lower back using a dough dam. Highly effective for lumbar disc problems, sciatica, and lower back pain.

Janu Basti

Warm medicated oil pooled over the knee joint. Specific treatment for knee arthritis, degeneration, and pain without requiring systemic enema.

Greeva Basti

Warm oil treatment for the cervical spine region. Used for cervical spondylosis, neck stiffness, and upper back pain.

Hrid Basti

Warm medicated oil over the heart region. Used for cardiac weakness, palpitations, and anxiety — deeply calming for the heart and mind.

The Basti Course — Karma Basti

A classical Karma Basti course consists of 30 Basti procedures administered over 30–45 days: alternating Anuvasana Basti (oil) and Niruha Basti (decoction). This sequence is prescribed for chronic, deep-seated Vata conditions.

Shorter courses — Yoga Basti (8 days) and Kala Basti (16 days) — are prescribed for less severe conditions. Dr. P. Murali Krishna will recommend the appropriate course length after assessment.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Is Basti (medicated enema) uncomfortable?
    Anuvasana Basti (oil) is generally very comfortable — patients often find it deeply relieving, especially for back and joint pain. Niruha Basti may cause mild cramping before expulsion, which is normal and temporary.
  • How is Basti different from a regular enema?
    A regular saline or water enema is used only for cleansing the rectum before procedures or for constipation relief. Ayurvedic Basti uses complex formulations of medicated oils, herbal decoctions, honey, and salts that are absorbed into the colon wall and bloodstream, producing systemic therapeutic effects throughout the body.
  • Can Basti cure arthritis permanently?
    Basti significantly reduces inflammation, pain, and stiffness in arthritis and in many cases produces long-lasting remission. Whether it provides permanent relief depends on the type and severity of arthritis, the patient's constitution, and adherence to post-treatment dietary and lifestyle guidelines.
  • Is Kati Basti the same as regular Basti?
    No. Kati Basti is a localised treatment where warm medicated oil is held over the lower back using a dough ring — it does not involve rectal administration. It is an external therapy used specifically for lumbar spine conditions.
