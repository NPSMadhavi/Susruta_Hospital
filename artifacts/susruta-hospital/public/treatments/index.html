Our Treatments

At Susruta Hospital, every treatment is prescribed after a thorough assessment using traditional Ayurvedic diagnostic methods — Nadi Pariksha (pulse diagnosis) and Prakriti analysis (body constitution). No two treatment plans are the same.

🌿

Panchakarma Therapy

The gold standard of Ayurvedic detoxification. Five systematic procedures to cleanse the body at the cellular level — Vamana, Virechana, Basti, Nasya, and Raktamokshana.

💧

Shirodhara

A continuous stream of warm medicated oil poured gently on the forehead. Deeply calming for the nervous system — effective for stress, insomnia, migraine, and anxiety.

🤲

Abhyanga

Full-body warm oil massage using specially prepared medicated oils. Improves circulation, nourishes tissues, relieves muscle stiffness, and promotes deep relaxation.

👃

Nasya Therapy

Medicated oil or herbal preparations administered through the nasal passage. Highly effective for sinusitis, headaches, facial paralysis, and neurological conditions.

🌾

Basti (Medicated Enema)

The most powerful Panchakarma procedure for Vata disorders. Medicated oils or herbal decoctions administered rectally to cleanse the colon and balance Vata dosha.

🍃

Ayurvedic Consultations

Detailed personal consultation using Nadi Pariksha and Prakriti analysis. Includes personalised herbal medicines, dietary advice, and lifestyle recommendations.
